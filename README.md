# Sales Performance & Market Analysis

## 1. Business Problem

A multi-category retail business operates across five cities and sells products across **Electronics, Clothing, and Groceries**.

Management needs a consolidated view of sales performance to understand where revenue is being generated, which products and categories are contributing most to sales, how performance differs across markets, and how revenue changes throughout the year.

The analysis focuses on the following business questions:

* Which categories and products are driving revenue?
* Which cities contribute the most and least revenue?
* Are high-volume products also generating high revenue?
* How does revenue change throughout the year?
* Which products, categories, and markets warrant further commercial attention?

---

## 2. Steps of Analysis

### Data Preparation

* Prepared transaction-level sales data for analysis in Power BI.
* Standardized data types and validated sales fields.
* Created month-level fields to support time-based analysis.
* Developed DAX measures for core sales KPIs.

### KPI Analysis

Calculated the following key performance indicators:

* **Total Revenue**
* **Total Orders**
* **Units Sold**
* **Average Order Value (AOV)**
* **Revenue per Unit**

### Sales Trend Analysis

Analyzed monthly revenue throughout **2025** to identify periods of stronger and weaker sales performance.

### Category Analysis

Compared revenue contribution across:

* Electronics
* Clothing
* Groceries

### Market Analysis

Compared revenue performance across five cities to identify differences in market contribution.

### Product Analysis

Analyzed products based on:

* Revenue
* Units Sold
* Revenue per Unit
* Revenue Contribution

A scatter analysis was used to evaluate the relationship between **sales volume and revenue**.

### Interactive Analysis

Added **Category, City, and Date** filters to allow users to investigate performance at different levels of detail.

---

## 3. Data Insights

The analysis provides visibility into:

* Overall revenue and sales volume across the business.
* Revenue contribution from each product category.
* Differences in revenue performance across cities.
* Products generating the highest revenue and sales volume.
* The relationship between product volume and revenue generation.
* Monthly fluctuations in revenue throughout 2025.
* Product-level revenue contribution and revenue per unit.

The dashboard allows these insights to be explored dynamically using **Category, City, and Date** filters.

---

## 4. Recommendations

Based on the observed sales patterns, management can use the analysis to:

* Prioritize high-contributing products and categories for continued commercial attention.
* Investigate lower-performing cities to understand potential differences in market performance.
* Review high-volume products that generate comparatively lower revenue.
* Monitor stronger and weaker sales periods to support sales planning.
* Evaluate product and category performance at the city level before making market-specific decisions.
* Use the dashboard as a recurring sales-performance monitoring tool.
